On Zanzibar the TCRA unit is a shehia: one postcode covers several shehia, so a TCRA ward there is a group and each shehia is matched on its own.

Postal districts and councils are not one-to-one: a TCRA district can hold several councils (a rural council and a town council
split from it) and the other way round. `districts.csv` lists every pair that wards link, with `shared_units` showing how many.

## Unmatched (59 TCRA, 154 NBS 2022)

All rows are in `crosswalk/unmatched.csv` with the closest name on the other side. Causes, most common first:

- **Ward splits and new wards since the TCRA list.** NBS 2022 counts 3,956 mainland wards, the TCRA list 3,921. NBS wards with no TCRA ward are mostly wards created after the postcode list.
- **Renamed wards and spelling.** Names that differ by more than the fuzzy threshold.
- **Pseudo-districts.** TCRA's CBD districts (for example Arusha CBD) are postal areas of a city council and have no council of their own; their wards link to the city council.
- **Zanzibar shehia.** The postcode source names shehia, not postal areas; NBS lists 388 shehia against 328 in the postcode source.

## Zanzibar region codes

TCRA allocated 71 Mjini Magharibi, 72 Kusini Unguja, 73 Kaskazini Unguja, 74 Kusini Pemba, 75 Kaskazini Pemba
(National Postcode Allocation Plan, June 2024, Table 4). `scripts/build_zanzibar.py` fails if any postcode in the source does not start with the
code of its region, and it did not fail: every Zanzibar postcode is under its TCRA region code.

Things to confirm with TCRA or the Office of the Chief Government Statistician (Zanzibar):

- The postcode source gives one postcode per group of shehia and no postal area names, so the Zanzibar `ward` is a joined list of shehia names. Replace it when TCRA publishes ward names.
- The TCRA plan lists some Zanzibar post offices that are not in the postcode source (for example Wete 75100, Mahonda 73200, Jambiani 72150): only the codes are checked, not the names.

## Fixes to the TCRA data

Each change made while cleaning `location-files/` is one row in [`data-fixes.csv`](data-fixes.csv) (file, line before the fix, rule, row before and after, evidence).
`scripts/clean_data.py` made them and can be read to see each rule.

| Rule | Rows changed | What it fixed |
|---|---|---|
| R1 whitespace | 1883 | line breaks and repeated spaces inside names |
| R2 fill-down code | 69 | ward codes counted up on every row of a spreadsheet column; reset to the ward's own code |
| R3 fill-down name | 45 | ward names with a counter filled down (kept the NBS 2022 name) |
| R4 district | 32 | ward codes not under their district: Madaba (Ruvuma) and Njisi (Mbeya), confirmed by NBS 2022 |
| R5 duplicate | 124 | exact duplicate rows removed |

The 173 issues in the old `scripts/known-data-issues.txt` baseline (116 duplicate rows, 32 ward codes not under their district, 25 codes with more than one name) are all resolved and the baseline is now empty.

NBS 2022 was the tiebreaker in R3 and R4. Where NBS 2022 did not settle a case, the first row was kept and the case is in `data-fixes.csv`.

## NBS 2022 extraction

The names in `sources/nbs2022/` are read from the PDFs and checked against the totals printed in the reports (ward sums per council, council sums per region, region sums per country; see `scripts/extract_nbs.py`). One misprint in the report was corrected: the council printed as `Nsimbo District l` is `Nsimbo District`.
